Make every word count.

Typography is more than choosing a font: it is the visual organization of language.

01

Hierarchy

Guide the eye from headline to supporting details using scale and weight.

02

Contrast

Combine size, weight, and style to create emphasis without clutter.

03

Spacing

Use line height and letter spacing to make content comfortable to read.

04

Alignment

Consistent edges and grids create visual order.
