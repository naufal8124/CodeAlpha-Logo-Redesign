03 / CASE STUDY

From user need to screen flow.

A concise UX case study outlining the audience, user journey, and design decisions.

01

Problem

Plant owners may forget care schedules or feel unsure how much light and water plants need.

02

Audience

Beginners and busy plant owners who want quick, reassuring guidance.

03

User flow

Open app → view dashboard → select a plant → read care instructions → set reminder.

04

Success criteria

Users can find a plant, understand its next care task, and return to their garden easily.

Tools and deliverables

Suggested tools: Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or InVision. Deliverables: wireframes, high-fidelity screens, a prototype flow, and a short usability rationale.
